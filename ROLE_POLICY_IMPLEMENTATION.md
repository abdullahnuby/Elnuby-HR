# ELNUBY HR Role Policy

- SYSTEM_ADMIN: full CRUD/system control from the application UI.
- HR_MANAGER: company-wide HR operations; no SYSTEM_ADMIN security controls.
- SECTOR_MANAGER: scoped project/sector management; NO attendance/check-in/check-out.
- PROJECT_MANAGER: company employee with attendance/check-in/check-out and scoped project management.
- EMPLOYEE: self-service attendance and requests.
- SITE_SUPERVISOR is retired and is not accepted by the application.

The System Administration Center provides CRUD for the supported HR business tables through the protected backend.
